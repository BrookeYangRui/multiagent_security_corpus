# Adjacent and Contextual Corpus

`adjacent_contextual.csv` contains structured works whose source review
recommends `security_relevant` or `adjacent` at paper level. These works may
support scope boundaries, formal background, related work, or sensitivity
analysis but do not enter the strict security denominator.

Claim-level exceptions remain possible. For example, a mixed safety paper may
contain one directly adversarial claim eligible for a claim-level analysis.
