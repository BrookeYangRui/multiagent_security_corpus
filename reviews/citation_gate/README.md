# Citation-Gate Reviews

`non_peer_gt10_full_text_adjudication.csv` records the primary-source review of
the 19 works that previously remained pending after the strict non-peer
`citationCount > 10` retrieval gate.

The citation threshold triggers full-text review; it does not imply inclusion.
Every row records the five security-scope gates, the accessed version, evidence
locations, a proposed scope, and whether author signoff is still required.

The corresponding generated candidate exports are under `corpus/final/`.
