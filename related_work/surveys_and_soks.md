# Multi-Agent Security SoKs and Comparators

The authoritative package supplies a 14-work supporting synthesis view in
`sok_related/papers.csv`. Seven of those works are also canonical academic
records, so 14 is never added to the 142-work corpus denominator.

## Direct and Closely Related Views

The relation and centrality fields distinguish direct multi-agent security
agendas, cross-domain perspectives, governance reviews, framework studies, and
narrow causal lenses. Each record links to its note under `papers/surveys/` and
to a primary source.

Direct or MAS-centered examples include:

- `schroederdewitt2025openchallenges`
- `ko2026sevenchallenges`
- `raza2026trism`
- `nguyen2026securityconsiderations`
- `shi2025trustauthorization`

## Broader Comparators

The view also retains the package's closest agentic-security, MCP-security,
trustworthy-agent, and general LLM-agent survey comparators. These records
provide neighboring taxonomies but do not independently establish empirical
multi-agent attack, defense, or prevalence claims.

## Use in Synthesis

- Cite a paper note or BibTeX key for every substantive claim.
- Use survey statements as synthesis context, not primary attack evidence.
- Deduplicate the seven corpus overlaps before reporting any combined count.
- Keep direct MAS-centered works distinct from broader comparators by using
  `relation_level` and `multiagent_security_centrality`.
